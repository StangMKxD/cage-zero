Following the Owner's explicit grilling confirmation (**“ได้ ยืนยัน”**), `agycage` authored and verified the five scoped skill animation revisions in accordance with `HANDOFF §Q` and [designs/skill-animation-rework-20261024.md](designs/skill-animation-rework-20261024.md):

1. **`HeavyKnockOn` (v02):**
   - Knocks the ball far into open space ahead (`ballImpulseMagnitude = 45.0`, initial lead offset 4.0 studs).
   - Character takes ONE short modest step forward (`playerVelocityBurst = 22.0`, `burstDurationSec = 0.18`) and does NOT chase all the way to the ball.
   - Ball travels visibly much farther than the player burst, leaving open space to contest; tackleability preserved.
   - `totalDurationSec = 0.36`, `contactTimeSec = 0.12`.

2. **`OpenUpFakeShot` (v02):**
   - Readable shot windup feint, open hips, real diagonal-forward ball touch (`contactDiagonal = [0.70, 0.70]`), short automatic following step (`playerVelocityBurst = 20.0`, `burstDurationSec = 0.22`).
   - Distinct from core `E` FakeShot.
   - `ballImpulseMagnitude = 22.0`, `totalDurationSec = 0.38`, `contactTimeSec = 0.16`.

3. **`BodyFeint` (v02):**
   - Shoulder/hip drop sharply in false direction opposite exit while ball stays near feet (0..0.18s).
   - Rapid weight transfer across body, contacting foot touches ball toward actual exit, short exit step (`playerVelocityBurst = 20.0`, `burstDurationSec = 0.22`).
   - `ballImpulseMagnitude = 22.0`, `totalDurationSec = 0.36`, `contactTimeSec = 0.18`.

4. **`HeelChop` (v02):**
   - **NO JUMP.** Superseded jump/straddle wording removed; RootJoint stays grounded (`RootJoint.Position.Y <= 0`).
   - Side chosen at activation:
     - **A / Left:** RIGHT foot itself cuts/hooks around left/support leg, sending ball left beside body.
     - **D / Right:** LEFT foot itself cuts/hooks around right/support leg, sending ball right beside body.
   - Grounded leg spread and cross-body weight shift near starting position; **BODY DOES NOT PURSUE BALL (`playerVelocityBurst = 0.0`, `burstDurationSec = 0.0`)**.
   - `ballImpulseMagnitude = 26.0`, `totalDurationSec = 0.36`, `contactTimeSec = 0.14`.

5. **`Elastico` (v03):**
   - Continuous two contacts on the **SAME foot** without artificial pause: outside of foot nudges ball outward (0.12s), same foot instantly snaps inward (0.24s) cutting ball across body.
   - Body weight follows true exit with one short decisive step (`playerVelocityBurst = 22.0`, `burstDurationSec = 0.22`).
   - Opposite variant mirrors foot/body/ball path.
   - `ballImpulseMagnitude = 26.0`, `totalDurationSec = 0.44`, `contactTimeSec = 0.12`.

## 2. Additional Approved Owner Directives

1. **Zero Cooldown / Pure Stamina Gating:**
   - Skills have 0 cooldown (`cooldownSec = 0.0`, `commonRecoverySec = 0.0`), governed purely by stamina per Owner directive ("และ skill ไม่มี cooldown อิงตาม stamina เลย กดได้ตลอด").
2. **Pass (E) Gauge Charge Speed Scaling:**
   - Added `CHARGE_TIME_MAX_PASS = 0.45` and `CHARGE_TIME_MAX_SHOOT = 0.90` to `src/shared/Constants.luau`.
   - Pass (E) reaches 100% max charge in 0.45s (2x faster than shoot/curve at 0.90s), verified live in Studio.
3. **Double Rotation Bug Resolution:**
   - Reverted `SkillMoveConfigs.ResolveCharacterRelativeDirection` to strictly return `forward, side` (`(Vector3, number)`), eliminating double-rotation that previously produced negative Z ball velocities in lateral cuts.
4. **EA Sports FC 27 Touch-Based Dribble State Machine (Zero Wobble / Full Freedom):**
   - Completely eliminated artificial lateral oscillation/swaying ("มันโยกไปมาๆ"): touches now drive strictly along `moveDir` in a clean, natural forward roll.
   - Implemented authentic 5-state dribbling architecture per Owner directive:
     1. **`IdleWithBall`:** When stationary (`speed <= 0.8`), ball is trapped resting calmly at player's feet (2.0 studs).
     2. **`NormalDribble` (Left Stick Dribble):** Touch cooldown 0.35s, gentle tap along `moveDir` (`speed * 1.10 + 0.8`), ball leads 2.5 - 3.2 studs with natural turf friction. Zero lateral wobble.
     3. **`SprintKnock` (Full Sprint Knock):** Touch cooldown 0.50s, knocks ball forward into open space (5.0 - 6.5 studs), character chases ball into space.
     4. **`CloseControl` (Agile Dribbling / L1 / Ball Roll):** Holding `Ctrl` or `Q` slows walk speed to 10.0 studs/s, touch frequency every 0.15s, tight lead < 2.0 studs, supports smooth lateral ball roll along `RightVector` when strafing.
     5. **`DragBack` (การดึงบอลกลับหลัง):** Moving backward (`moveDir:Dot(flatFwd) < -0.45` or pressing `S`) while in Close Control or tapping `S + Ctrl/Z`, halts ball velocity immediately and draws ball smoothly rearward for 0.25s to pivot.
     6. **`OffBalanceDribbling`:** Passive body contact from opponents causes carrier to stumble/slow down rather than instant ball loss; carrier retains touch control unless actively tackled (`Poke`/`Slide`/`CagePress`) or contact exceeds 0.45s.
   - User directive: NO Controlled Sprint (R1) added.
