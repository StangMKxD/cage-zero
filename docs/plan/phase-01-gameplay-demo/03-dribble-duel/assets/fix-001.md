2. **5 Skill Moves Catalog, 5-Star Starter Ceiling & 3-Slot Loadout (`T-01`):**
   - Created `src/shared/configs/SkillMoveConfigs.luau` defining:
     - `HeavyKnockOn` (1★): Stamina `0.15`, Cooldown `1.8s`, Duration `0.35s`, Common Recovery `0.20s`, forward ball knock (`38.0`) + sprint burst (`28.0` for `0.25s`).
     - `DragBackTurn` (2★): Stamina `0.18`, Cooldown `2.2s`, Duration `0.50s`, Common Recovery `0.22s`, backward drag (`-24.0`) + 180° character pivot.
     - `LaCroqueta` (3★): Stamina `0.22`, Cooldown `2.8s`, Duration `0.45s`, Common Recovery `0.22s`, two-foot lateral shift (right inside foot -> left inside foot) + diagonal exit.
     - `SombreroFlick` (4★): Stamina `0.28`, Cooldown `3.8s`, Duration `0.55s`, Common Recovery `0.28s`, high overhead parabolic lob (`forward 14.0`, `vertical 38.0`, `BALL_ARC_MODE_HIGH_LOB`) distinct from `BallPop`, allowing ground `Slide` tackles to pass underneath without intercepting the overhead ball.
     - `Elastico` (5★): Stamina `0.32`, Cooldown `4.5s`, Duration `0.50s`, Common Recovery `0.30s`, outside-right feint + sharp inside-right diagonal snap (`30.0`).
   - Default 3-slot loadout (`slot1 = HeavyKnockOn`, `slot2 = LaCroqueta`, `slot3 = SombreroFlick`) with starter `starCeiling = 5` (no Gacha).
   - `SkillMoveService.SetPlayerLoadout` validates star ceiling, rejects duplicate skills across slots, and blocks loadout swaps during an active match (`isStoppedOrResetContext == false`) or active skill/recovery window.

3. **Skill Move Lifecycle, Single Ball Writer & Procedural R6 Poses (`T-02`, `T-04`):**
   - `SkillMoveService.luau` validates `Attacking` context, personal possession (`BallService.GetPossessorUserId() == userId`), `characterGeneration`, `contextVersion`, `intentId` deduplication, per-skill cooldown (`skillCooldowns[skillId]`), and delegates ball/character physics exclusively to `BallService.ApplySkillBallImpulse` (single ball writer).
   - Per-skill cooldowns and common recovery windows (`commonSkillRecoveryUntil`) persist across `SetPlayerLoadout`, `BallService.ResetBall`, and `BallService.SetPlayerTeamSide` (anti-bypass verified in `TC-P03-09`).
   - Added procedural R6 poses in `PlayerActionPoseConfigs.GetSkillMovePose` and `GetTacklePose` with analytical OBB floor clearance (`ApplyClearanceOffset`), wired into `R6ActionPoseController` (`TriggerSkillMove`, `TriggerTackle`). `LaCroqueta` uses both right and left feet; all other skills and tackles remain right-foot dominant.

4. **Real-Time Defense (`Slide` Reuse, `Poke`, `Shoulder`) & Contact Physics (`T-03`):**
   - **Slide Tackle (`LMB` in `Defending`):** Reused from Plan 02 with vertical clearance check (`slideVertDelta <= 3.0`) so ground slides pass underneath high airborne `SombreroFlick` arcs while preserving `TC-P02-35` and `TC-P02-36`.
   - **Quick Poke (`RMB` in `Defending`):** Reach `4.5` studs, stamina `0.10`, cooldown `1.0s`, duration `0.28s`, whiff recovery `0.20s`. Cleanly steals possession on receivable ground/low balls (`relY <= 1.5` and `speed <= 42.0`, including mid-skill attackers since skills have **no I-frames**), or knocks fast/high balls free.
   - **Shoulder Charge (`MMB` ONLY in `Defending`):** Reach `3.5` studs, stamina `0.14`, cooldown `2.0s`, duration `0.35s`, whiff recovery `0.25s`. Slows hit opponent by 40% (`WalkSpeed * 0.60` for `0.40s`) and dislodges the ball into **`Free`** state (`possessorUserId = nil`, **never** auto-awarded to the charger).
   - **Ordinary Body Contact vs Teammate Pass-Through:** In `BallService.StepPhysics` & `ResolvePlayerSpeed`, opponent body contact (`<= 3.2` studs) applies a `0.80x` contact slow without stripping possession, whereas teammates pass through with zero slow (`TC-P03-12`).

5. **Retirement of Legacy Choice Duel Modules (`T-05`):**
   - Retired `src/server/services/DuelEngineService.luau`, `src/client/controllers/DuelUIController.luau`, and `src/client/ui/screens/DuelChoiceModal.luau` (`IsRetired = true`, no-op `Init`/`StartDuel`/`OpenModal`, non-blocking `FindFirstChild` for legacy `ChoiceConfigs`/`ParryService`, zero active listeners on `DuelChoiceRequest` / `DuelStateNotify`).
