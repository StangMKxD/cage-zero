### 1.1 Owner Adaptation § 15 (State-Based Momentum Jump & Soft Apex Timing)
1. **Central Flight FSM (`Types.FlightState`):**
   - States: `Grounded` $\to$ `Takeoff` $\to$ `Rising` $\to$ `Apex` $\to$ `Falling` $\to$ `Landed` $\to$ `Grounded`.
   - Pure deterministic transitions via `JumpModel.ResolveFlightState(vy, timeSinceTakeoff, hasLeftGround, isGrounded)`.
   - Guarded against false ground hits by requiring `timeSinceTakeoff > 0.12s` and true non-air floor contact before transitioning to `Landed`.
2. **Single Instantaneous Launch Seam (Zero Physics Conflict):**
   - Eliminated stacking forces from native `Humanoid.Jump` / `ChangeState(Jumping)`. Native `JumpPower` is set to `0` and native jumping state is disabled.
   - Single instantaneous physical impulse `ApplyImpulse(mass * deltaV)` is applied at takeoff on the client network owner for real player characters (`VERTICAL_LAUNCH_SPEED = 78.0 studs/s`). Natural gravity (`196.2 studs/s²`) governs the flight arc thereafter.
   - Server applies impulse authoritatively for headless mock characters in automated tests.
3. **Discrete Spacebar Release & Debounce:**
   - Single Spacebar press triggers exactly one flight.
   - Holding Spacebar does not bunnyhop or auto-repeat. The player must physically release Spacebar and press again on the ground after landing lag expires.
4. **Captured Horizontal Momentum & Yaw Lock:**
   - Stationary jump ($XZ \le 0.08$): Horizontal speed is strictly locked to zero ($XZ = 0$).
   - Moving jump: Exact horizontal walk velocity ($16-20$ studs/s) or sprint velocity ($24$ studs/s) is captured at takeoff and locked throughout the flight without WASD steer drift.
   - Character yaw/facing remains locked to committed takeoff facing (`jumpFacingF0`).
5. **Camera & 3D Mouse-Aim Authority:**
   - Camera remains 100% FREE for 3D mouse aiming (retaining § 14 aim-relative base trajectory and bounded $\le 5^\circ$ lean deflection).
6. **Continuous Soft Apex Quality Curve ($[0.90, 1.00]$):**
   - Pure deterministic formulation in `JumpModel.CalculateSoftQuality(timeSinceTakeoff, currentVY)`:
     $$q = 0.90 + 0.10 \times \max\left(0, 1 - \frac{|\Delta t - T_{\text{apex}}|}{T_{\text{apex}}}\right)$$
   - Peaks at $T_{\text{apex}} = v_0 / g \approx 0.398\text{s}$ with quality $1.00$. Smoothly scales down to $0.90$ at takeoff ($0.0\text{s}$) and late falling.
   - Offensive actions (kicks/passes) are permitted throughout the entire flight (`Rising`, `Apex`, `Falling`) without hard-gating, but their launch speeds scale continuously by $q$.
7. **Airborne Offensive Action Budget:**
   - 1 valid manual offensive release per flight shared across `DirectShoot` (LMB), `Volley` (RMB), and `Pass` (MMB).
   - Subsequent releases in the same flight are authoritatively rejected with reason `"ExceededBudget"`.
   - Whiffing an airborne kick consumes the flight budget without applying ground walking recovery stun. Budget resets upon landing.
8. **Landing Lag Debounce:**
   - $0.08\text{s}$ debounce window upon touchdown. Re-jumping during this window is rejected with reason `"LandingLag"`.
   - Landing lag does NOT stun or impede ground walk speed, sprint speed, or ground kicking.

### 1.2 Owner Delta § 16 (Grounded Possessor Space = BallPop)
1. **Contextual Gate Before Character Launch:**
   - When Space is pressed while **grounded and personally possessing the ball** (`isPossessor == true` and `isGrounded == true`):
     - Character performs **`BallPop`**.
     - **THE CHARACTER STAYS 100% ON THE GROUND.** No impulse applied to character, no `Humanoid.Jump`, no `Takeoff`/`Rising` flight state, no `flightId`, no `airOffensiveBudget`, no `jumpFacingF0` lock, no `landingLag`.
     - The player can freely continue walking or sprinting with WASD.
2. **Deterministic Ball Launch Velocity & Gravity Parity:**
   - Reference character jump apex rise height:
     $$H = \frac{v_{\text{jump0}}^2}{2 \cdot g_{\text{character}}} = \frac{78^2}{2 \times 196.2} \approx 15.5046\text{ studs}$$
   - Existing ball gravity: $g_{\text{ball}} = 65.0\text{ studs/s²}$ (unmodified existing ball physics).
   - Required ball launch vertical velocity:
     $$V_{y,\text{ball}} = \sqrt{2 \cdot g_{\text{ball}} \cdot H} = \sqrt{2 \times 65.0 \times 15.5046} = \sqrt{2015.6} \approx 44.895\text{ studs/s}$$
   - Horizontal velocity:
     - Idle / stationary ($|\text{WASD}| < 0.08$): Pops nearly straight up ($XZ = 0$). Does not force forward speed.
     - Moving: Horizontal velocity along normalized WASD move direction matching ground walk ($20$) or sprint ($24$) speed.
3. **Single Ball Writer & Atomic State Transition:**
   - `BallService.ExecuteBallPop` atomically releases possession:
     - `ballState.possessorUserId = nil`
     - `ballState.state = "Free"`
     - `ballState.inFlightAction = "BallPop"`
     - `ballState.velocity = launchVelocity`
     - `ballState.lastPossessingTeam = st.teamSide` (preserves attacking team context)
     - `ballState.lastKickerUserId = userId`, `ballState.lastKickTime = os.clock()`
4. **Re-acquire Guard:**
   - Re-acquire cooldown window for `BallPop` set to $0.35\text{s}$ (`BALL_POP_REPOSSESS_COOLDOWN`), preventing the kicker from instantly self-repossessing the ball at their feet while it rises into the air.
   - Other players (teammates and opponents) can contact or contest the ball immediately without penalty.
5. **Space Sequence: Release + Second Space Press Jumps to Follow Ball:**
   - After Space is physically released, the ball is free.
   - Pressing Space AGAIN triggers the normal custom momentum jump (§ 15), allowing the player to leap into the air and strike/volley the popping ball in mid-air.
6. **Negative & Edge Cases Handled:**
   - Grounded possessor calling `DirectionalJump` is authoritatively rejected with reason `"PossessingBall"`.
   - Non-possessor Space (ball free, teammate owns ball, or enemy owns ball) performs a normal directional jump; teammate owning the ball is NOT personal possession.
   - BallPop attempted while airborne or without ball possession is rejected with `"Recovery"` / `"NotPossessor"`.
   - BallPop does not cancel an active charge or consume airborne offensive budget.
